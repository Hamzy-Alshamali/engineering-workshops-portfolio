Getting started and backups
  • Open this file directly in Chrome or Edge. No account or internet connection is needed.
  • Enter a task title, course, due date, and priority. Use the checkbox to complete or reopen a task.
  • An overdue task is incomplete and due before today, using your device’s local date.

  • Data is saved for this browser and file. Moving the file or using another browser may show a different collection. Export a backup to transfer your tasks.
