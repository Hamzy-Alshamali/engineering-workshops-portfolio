Task: one handwritten digit, classes 0–9.
Model: pretrained convolutional neural network. We use inference, not training.
Input: one 28×28 grayscale image; bright digit, black background; float32 values 0–1; tensor shape [1,1,28,28]. This model takes one image at a time.
Output: shape [1,10], raw class scores (logits), one for each digit in order 0–9. Use argmax for the predicted class. Use stable softmax for a displayed score; the score is not a guarantee.
Webcam adaptation: crop one digit; grayscale; separate ink/background; invert dark ink on white; bounding box; preserve aspect ratio; fit approximately 20×20; centre with black padding to 28×28. This preprocessing is part of the app, not new training.
Limitations: trained on MNIST, not general camera images. Shadows, tilted cards, printed fonts, multiple digits and clutter can cause errors. Evaluate on your own camera inputs.
